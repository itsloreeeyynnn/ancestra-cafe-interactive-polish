About Us

Welcome to Ancestra Cafe, a coffee shop nestled in one of Argao’s oldest homes, believed to date back to the 1890s. More than just a place to eat and drink, Ancestra is a celebration of Cebuano culture—a space where food, history, and community come together.

Our menu is inspired by Argao’s rich culinary traditions, crafted with local ingredients and heartfelt flavors. Whether you're seeking a quiet weekday retreat with a comforting cup of coffee or gathering with friends and family for a weekend feast, Ancestra offers a warm, meaningful experience.

We are committed to supporting local farmers, artisans, and entrepreneurs, helping nurture a sustainable and vibrant community. Through our food, events, and space, we honor the past while creating a place of connection for the present.

Come join us at Ancestra Cafe—where every visit is a celebration of heritage, heart, and home.

Where stories are shared

Step inside Ancestra Cafe and feel the comfort of a house steeped in memory. Designed with heritage in mind, our space blends local materials, vintage details, and cozy corners.

The name and its story

Where “Ancestra” came from

Our name “Ancestra” comes from two inspirations. First, it is the house - this ancestral home of the accomplished Kintanar family, whose rustic beauty sparked a dream of a cafe that honors it’s story.

But “Ancestra” also means something deeper. It is the spanish word for a female ancestor. The cafe is an homage to the women in our family who built businesses, raised generations, and shaped communities but are less celebrated than their male counterparts.

In Argao, there’s a well-known street named after our great-grandfather Don Gil Garcia. But behind him was Chichay- his wife, our great-grandmother - who was just as formidable a person and was the brains and energy behind the family businesses. No street bears her name, but her spirit is everywhere.

Then there’s Lola Piling (Pilar Alvarez Espina), a professional cook who once worked at a cafe on Mango Avenue. She passed down not just recipes, but a deep love for food and warmth that we carry with us today.

Ancestra is named for them. And for every woman whose legacy deserves to be celebrated at our table.
