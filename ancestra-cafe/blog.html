Where did “ancestra” come from?

The family and history behind the café’s name.

April 13, 2026 · Our story

Our name “Ancestra” comes from two inspirations. First, it is the house - this ancestral home of the accomplished Kintanar family, whose rustic beauty sparked a dream of a cafe that honors it’s story.

But “Ancestra” also means something deeper. It is the spanish word for a female ancestor. The cafe is an homage to the women in our family who built businesses, raised generations, and shaped communities but are less celebrated than their male counterparts.

In Argao, there’s a well-known street named after our great-grandfather Don Gil Garcia. But behind him was Chichay- his wife, our great-grandmother - who was just as formidable a person and was the brains and energy behind the family businesses. No street bears her name, but her spirit is everywhere.

Then there’s Lola Piling (Pilar Alvarez Espina), a professional cook who once worked at a cafe on Mango Avenue. She passed down not just recipes, but a deep love for food and warmth that we carry with us today.

Ancestra is named for them. And for every woman whose legacy deserves to be celebrated at our table.

June 25, 2025 · Guest information

Ancestra Cafe Guest Policies

Guidelines for a welcoming experience at the café.

June 25, 2025 · Guest information

To ensure a wonderful experience for all our guests, we kindly ask for your cooperation with the following guidelines:

Be Kind & Respectful: Please treat our staff and fellow customers with courtesy and respect. We foster a positive and inclusive environment for everyone.

Ordering & Payment: Ancestra is a fast-casual coffee shop. Please place your order at the counter pay ahead. Food will be served at your table. Water is self-service. We can only take dine-in orders if you already have a table. If you do not, we can serve takeout.

We accept Cash, GCash, Paymaya, and all major credit and debit cards.

Seating & Space: During busy hours, please be mindful of others waiting for a table. We kindly ask that laptop use be limited or tables shared if possible when we are at full capacity. Seats are only for customers.

  • Reservations: We do not take reservations for tables.
  • Personal Belongings: Please keep your personal belongings with you and avoid using extra seats or tables for them when space is limited.
  • Bathrooms are for customer use only.

Noise Levels: Help us maintain a pleasant ambiance. Please be mindful of your noise level, including conversations and phone calls. Use headphones if you’re listening to audio on your devices.

Cleanliness: Please leave all your used items on the table. Staff will clean up the table and clearing the table by customers is not required, though leaving the tables as clean and orderly as possible is appreciated. Please inform the staff as soon as possible if any spillage or breakage occurs so they can clean up quickly.

Outside Food & Drink: We kindly request that only food and beverages purchased in Ancestra be consumed on the premises.

Children: Children are welcome! For their safety and the comfort of other guests, please supervise your children at all times.

Pets: Well-behaved pets and service animals are welcome in the café as long as they are leashed. Please be mindful of their noise and behavior. If the animals make any kind of mess, it is the responsibility of the owner to clean up.

Smoking & Vaping: Ancestra is a smoke-free and vape-free environment, including our outdoor seating areas.

Wi-Fi Usage: We offer complimentary Wi-Fi for our guests. Please use it responsibly and fairly, refraining from large downloads or activities that may slow down the network for others. Connection details are available from our staff.

Safety & Security: Ancestra is not responsible for lost or stolen items. Please keep an eye on your valuables. We do keep a lost-and-found box behind the counter. Please describe lost items before they can be released to you. In case of an emergency, please follow the instructions of our staff.

Feedback & Concerns: We value your feedback! If you have any concerns or suggestions, please speak to a member of our staff or the manager on duty. You can also reach us at contact@ancestracafe.ph or our social media.

Right to Refuse Service: We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone who is disruptive, disrespectful, or violates our cafe policies, to ensure the safety and comfort of our staff and other guests.

Thank you for your understanding and for helping us make Ancestra Café a welcoming place for all!
Ancestra Management
